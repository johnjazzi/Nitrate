- (capability) CAP-1: RAW photo capture with manual controls (shutter, ISO, aperture), bypassing Apple processing
- (capability) CAP-2: Apple Log 2 video capture with same manual controls as photo
- (capability) CAP-3: Lock shutter or ISO independently; auto-expose unlocked parameter to target EV
- (capability) CAP-4: Apply film stock at capture or post-capture; swap stocks on saved clean captures
- (capability) CAP-5: Trim video clips in-app without re-encoding source Log footage
- (capability) CAP-6: LUT to grain to halation to glow processing pipeline
- (capability) CAP-7: ISO-driven grain with stock-accurate patterns; temporally naturalistic video grain
- (capability) CAP-8: Toggle to keep or discard original RAW/Log files
- (capability) CAP-9: /year or 0 lifetime purchase; no feature gating between tiers
- (capability) CAP-10: Default point-and-shoot experience; first photo within 5 seconds of launch
- (constraint) iOS-only v1; iPhone 12 Pro+ for RAW, older devices JPEG fallback
- (constraint) No in-app sharing beyond camera roll save
- (constraint) No custom LUT import v1; only bundled starter stocks
- (constraint) No multi-clip video editing; trim only
- (constraint) Seven starter stocks; no user-generated stock creation v1
- (constraint) Apple Log 2 requires iPhone 15 Pro or newer
- (decision) Non-goals: Android, multi-clip editing, community presets, DSLR RAW import, full editing suite, social sharing, custom LUTs
- (assumption) RAW means ProRAW on 12 Pro+; standard RAW fallback TBD during architecture
- (assumption) Seven accurate stocks sufficient for v1; not competing with Dehancer on count
- (assumption) Aperture control is simulated/virtual on supported iPhones; no physical variable aperture exists
- (question) What is the unpaid/trial experience? Watermark, limited exports, or full-featured with prompt?
- (question) Is film stock ISO slider per-shot or global per-stock?
- (question) Should video grain be pre-computed/baked or real-time during playback/export?
