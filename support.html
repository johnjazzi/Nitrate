Support

Help with Nitrate.

Contact

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, the app version from Settings, and what happened. Share a clip only if you want to and it contains nothing private.

Recording and rendering

Nitrate records in Apple Log 2 and applies the film look after capture, on your device. Capture requires a supported iPhone and iOS 27.

Purchases

Rendering is included during the evaluation period, then requires a subscription or a lifetime purchase. Recording is always free. Use Restore Purchases in Settings to restore a purchase made with your Apple Account.

Never send passwords, verification codes or payment details to support.

Your footage

If an export fails, check Storage & Queue before deleting anything. Do not uninstall the app as a first troubleshooting step — app-local media can be lost.
