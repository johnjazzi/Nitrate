# Low-input launch operations

This is the executable layer above [mood.camera research](mood-camera-research.md). Tasks and numeric gates below are **proposals**, not current traction or forecasts. Founder/mission copy remains author-written. No accounts were connected, posts sent or budget spent.

## Goal and order

Recruit compatible-device shooters who complete a real capture → render → Photos save and choose to shoot again. Do not optimize for a large invitation list before that works. Technical gates in [readiness](readiness.md) take precedence over marketing dates.

1. Owner's internal TestFlight build.
2. First eight external opt-in testers after applicable beta approval.
3. One community request, then at most twelve additional qualified testers.
4. Public release materials and App Store submission after reliability/second-use evidence.

## Owner input: batch once, approve in batches

**One setup packet:** OS/product-policy answers from readiness; Developer/App Store Connect access through normal authorization; support contact/privacy hosting; founder copy; permission to use specific clips; optionally names of known willing shooters. No passwords or private signing keys in chat.

**One filming session:** the authentic clip and screen-recording brief in [README/assets](readme-layout-and-assets.md). Use the actual candidate build. Agent can edit/resize/prepare variants from supplied material; it cannot substitute fabricated footage or claim the user shot it.

**Suggested weekly involvement after setup:** one 15–20 minute decision batch covering candidate fixes, outgoing draft batch, clip rights and go/no-go. Owner still tests visual quality and signs legal/commercial declarations. If this time box is exceeded, shrink recruitment rather than automate away unresolved consent or reliability.

Agent prepares and maintains the tracker, eligibility/feedback form, approved-facts sheet, candidate community rules, individually relevant draft invitations, per-build known issues and a weekly decision brief. After explicit approval and app authorization it can execute an approved set of invitations/posts and read back outcomes; until then these are drafts only. No unattended cold-DM campaign, fictitious identity, scraped private contacts or unapproved spend.

## Recruitment workflow

| Step | Agent prepares | Approval/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Private pilot | Up to 8 invite records using owner-provided contacts or people who explicitly opt in; eligible device/OS check | Owner approves recipients; tester opts in |
| Creator task | One 10–20 second clip, actual render/save and short response | Tester may decline media upload; app build and failure stage recorded |
| Community expansion | One candidate community's current self-promotion/beta rules, moderator permission if needed, relevant footage and device requirement | No community is marked approved until rules/permission are actually checked |
| Asset reuse | Named clip, channels, credit and removal route | Separate permission from beta participation; no assumed ad rights |
| Editorial/PH | Fact sheet, author copy, real demo, stable public link | Only after candidate is reliable and owner approves posting/pitching |

Initial channel hypothesis: personal filmmaker/camera contacts → one permission-checked iPhone-video community → voluntary tester-shared footage → selective editorial/product community. This adapts the observed community-first method; it is not evidence Nitrate has the same audience or acquisition economics.

## Minimal assets

- One owned final rendered clip, no extra grade added without disclosure.
- One actual UI-to-Photos walkthrough; clearly label time cuts/speed-ups.
- Still fallback/thumbnail and 2 short crops of the same authentic footage.
- Verified compatibility, current limitations, support and privacy links.
- Author headline/story/CTA, inserted verbatim on request.

Do not create App Store/TestFlight availability badges before the corresponding URL works. Do not represent film-stock names as a manufacturer endorsement.

## First four weeks (relative to technical gate clearance)

| Window | Action | Output / stop-go |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Complete internal TestFlight matrix; collect owned demo; approve first invite batch | Zero open blocking media/privacy failures; actual processed build installed |
| Week 2 | Invite 8 qualified testers; task check at 48h, follow-up at day 3 and day 7 | Target 6 responses; at least 5/6 finish without guided rescue and 4/6 want another shoot |
| Week 3 | If gate passes, one rule-approved community request; up to 12 new opt-ins | Diagnose eligibility/installation/export friction; no broad promotion if reliability worsens |
| Week 4 | Review second-use and permissioned clips; decide public candidate vs another fix cycle | Proposed threshold: 6/8 respondents activate and 4/8 shoot on a second day; two reliable beta builds before broad launch |

These small cohorts are diagnostic, not statistically significant. Also report all-invited denominators, eligibility exclusions and nonresponse. A favorable responder-only fraction must not hide low activation across the cohort.

**Immediate stop:** lost original, unrecoverable saved take, repeated production crash, privacy surprise or unsupported hardware represented as supported. Pause new invitations, preserve diagnostic evidence with permission, fix and retest with affected testers. Do not buy reach around a broken funnel.

**Budget:** $0 distribution spend by default. Any paid asset logistics, ads or creator fee needs a separate approval. No forecast of virality, revenue, downloads or cost per acquisition.

## Tracking and feedback

Use `cohort-tracker.csv` with pseudonymous tester IDs; keep actual contact details in a private local/account system, never commit them to this public repository. Do not store feedback footage or identifiable comments in this repository. The template has no real people.

- Activation: tester reports a successful real-device capture → render → Photos save within 48 hours.
- Second use: tester reports another shooting day within 7 days, without treating reminder response as a shoot.
- Quality: failure stage, build/device/OS, permission/storage context; asset upload optional and separately consented.
- Share signal: volunteered sharing and permissioned clips, not incentivized reviews or forced promotion.
- Use Apple-provided beta diagnostics honestly; don't describe all beta operations as data-free merely because the app lacks a custom analytics client. The research packet links Apple's tester privacy information.

Three short questions, not a survey: **Did you get a saved result? Did you shoot again? What stopped you or made you reach for it?** For a defect, request only the minimum reproduction/diagnostics needed. Never ask for sensitive personal media by default.

## Weekly decision brief format

1. Candidate SHA/build and known blocking issues.
2. Eligible / invited / installed / activated / responded / second-use counts; unknowns retained.
3. Top three reproduced failures and fix verification.
4. Up to one outgoing batch and one asset selection for approval.
5. Go / hold / narrow scope with one reason.

## Draft invitation structure — not founder copy

`[why this recipient's shooting is relevant] → [author's invitation] → [verified device/OS] → [one short test task] → [known limitation] → [approved beta link] → [feedback/privacy route]`.

Do not fill the author voice, send the template, publish a community post or attribute approval without a real approval record.
