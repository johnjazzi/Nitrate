Quiet controls.

Flat charcoal faces, one soft green selection color. No raised rims, bevels or nested panels. The frame does the work.

Stock colors stay visible, with the gauge alongside and mm beneath its number. Tap the stock or gauge to choose stock, gauge and push/pull together.

Follows the selected stock, not the light in the room.

P1 / LENS PLACEMENT

Four compact 30px bubbles: 13, 24, 96 and F, with invisible 44px tap targets. Selection stays in one tight row above the shutter. Top-right placement keeps the row out of the shooting area and opens the same choices.

P2 / LESS HARDWARE

Measured exposure runs along the bottom of the preview as a fine horizontal ruler. Recording status sits directly on the image. Remaining time becomes a small type-only readout, not another panel.

D / V / P

A compact capsule with one centered selection and neighboring modes just visible. Swipe, use arrow keys, or tap a neighboring letter.
