Privacy Policy

Effective EFFECTIVE_DATE_HERE

This policy explains how Nitrate handles your information. Nitrate is a camera and film-emulation app for iPhone.

Your recordings

Video and audio are captured and processed on your device. Nitrate does not upload your footage to a server operated by the developer.

Saving a clip to Photos adds it to your photo library. Your device settings, including iCloud Photos, may sync that copy independently of Nitrate. If you remove an item from Nitrate's queue, keep in mind that exported copies may still exist in Photos. Keep separate backups of footage you want to keep.

Permissions

  • Camera — to record video.
  • Microphone — to record audio with your video.
  • Photos — to add rendered clips to your library (add-only).

You can change any permission in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Nitrate checks verified purchase entitlements on your device. Nitrate does not operate a subscriber database or a receipt server.

Settings and evaluation

Nitrate stores app preferences and the local evaluation start date on your device.

Advertising and analytics

Nitrate contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and declares no tracking.

If you contact support

If you email support, your message and any attachment are used to respond to your request. Only send what you are comfortable sharing.

Children

Nitrate is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's data handling changes. The effective date above reflects the current version.
