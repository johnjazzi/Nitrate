Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Nitrate is a camera and film-emulation app for iPhone, provided by John Azzinaro. This policy covers the app, this website, and information you choose to send to support.

Your recordings

Video and audio are captured and processed on your device. Nitrate does not upload your footage to a server operated by the developer.

Saving a clip to Photos adds it to your photo library. Your device settings, including iCloud Photos, may sync that copy independently of Nitrate. If you remove an item from Nitrate's queue, keep in mind that exported copies may still exist in Photos. Keep separate backups of footage you want to keep.

Permissions

  • Camera — to record video.
  • Microphone — to record audio with your video.
  • Photos — to add rendered clips to your library (add-only).

You can change any permission in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Nitrate checks verified purchase entitlements on your device. Nitrate does not operate a subscriber database or a receipt server.

Settings and evaluation

Nitrate stores app preferences and the local evaluation start date on your device.

Advertising and analytics

Nitrate contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and declares no tracking.

If you contact support

If you email support, your email address, message, and any attachments are used to respond to your request and handle related follow-up. Only send what you are comfortable sharing. Email services process messages as part of delivery; do not send passwords, verification codes, or full payment details.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle your request, related follow-up, and applicable legal obligations. You can request access, correction, or deletion by emailing nitrate.camera@johnazz.dev. Information that must be retained for legal reasons may be kept for that purpose.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed in. This is separate from the on-device processing of your footage. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data collection for the host’s practices.

Local data and deletion

Your recordings and app settings remain on your device until you remove them. Manage app-local footage in Storage & Queue and exported clips separately in Photos. Deleting a queue entry does not necessarily delete every original or exported copy. Deleting the app can remove app-local footage and settings, so back up anything you want to keep first. Photos copies, device backups, and iCloud copies are managed separately through Apple’s services and your device settings.

You can revoke camera, microphone, or Photos permissions in iPhone Settings. Nitrate does not have a separate app account to delete. Apple manages purchase records under its own terms and policies.

Children

Nitrate is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's data handling changes. The effective date above reflects the current version.
